Research checklist

  • Search marketplaces for existing offers in your niche
  • Read 10+ reviews — note the exact language your audience uses
  • Identify what's missing or unclear in existing products
  • Confirm people are already paying for something like this
  • Complete your transformation statement before moving on

AI prompts

Audience research prompt
Act as a market research analyst. I'm creating a digital product for [AUDIENCE] about [TOPIC]. Analyze what problems they struggle with most, what language they use to describe those problems, what solutions already exist, and what gaps are most common. Give me 5 pain points, 5 phrases they likely use, and 3 product angles I could take.
Transformation statement generator
Write 5 transformation statements for a digital product using this format: "This product helps [WHO] go from [PAIN STATE] to [DESIRED RESULT] without [COMMON OBSTACLE]." My audience is [AUDIENCE], my topic is [TOPIC], their pain is [PAIN], the outcome is [OUTCOME], and the obstacle to avoid is [OBSTACLE]. Make each feel specific, believable, and outcome-focused.
02

Design

Build your framework first. AI fills in the language later. You are the architect.

Structure beats volume every time. Design your product as stages, pillars, or steps — then use AI to expand each. Your structure becomes proprietary.

Proven structures to borrow

5-step system3-pillar modelBefore / after bridgeProblem → solution → resultStages of transformation

Scope checklist

  • Define exactly what's in vs. out of this product
  • One product = one transformation — don't cram everything in
  • Eliminate before you add — ruthless scope discipline
  • Can someone finish this in one sitting?
  • Start small — validate, then expand

AI prompts

Framework design prompt
I'm creating a [PRODUCT_TYPE] that helps [AUDIENCE] go from [PAIN] to [OUTCOME]. Design a named framework for this product with 4–6 stages or pillars. For each stage, give it a memorable name, a one-sentence description, and the key action the buyer takes. Make it feel proprietary and memorable — like something I could name and brand.
Outline generator prompt
Create a complete product outline for a [PRODUCT_TYPE] on [TOPIC] for [AUDIENCE]. Include section names, what each section teaches or delivers, and what the buyer can do after completing it. Structure it so there's clear progression from confusion to confidence. Keep it tight — prioritize outcomes over volume.
03

Build

You outline → AI drafts → you refine → AI polishes → you format. Repeat.

The AI build loop

You outline→ AI drafts→ You refine→ AI polishes→ You format
Your job is to edit with judgment — cut fluff, add nuance, verify facts, and test the product as your buyer would.

Build checklist

  • Draft each section using the section drafter prompt below
  • Cut anything that doesn't move the buyer forward
  • Add your own nuance, examples, and voice
  • Verify any facts AI generates — don't blindly trust output
  • Test the product as your buyer would before packaging

AI prompts

Section drafter prompt
Write the content for [SECTION NAME] of my [PRODUCT_TYPE] on [TOPIC]. My audience is [AUDIENCE] and their biggest struggle with this section is [PAIN]. Include: a brief explanation, a concrete example, 3 actionable steps, and a reflection question. Tone: warm, direct, practical — not academic. Write for someone who is smart but time-poor.
Multi-version intro generator
Write 3 versions of the introduction for my [PRODUCT_TYPE] on [TOPIC]: one story-driven, one problem-first, and one results-first. My buyer is [AUDIENCE] and the outcome they get is [OUTCOME]. I'll combine the best elements from each version.
Worksheet builder prompt
Create a 1-page worksheet for the [SECTION NAME] section of my product. Include: a brief context sentence, 3–5 fill-in-the-blank prompts, 2 short-answer questions, and a "done when" completion checkbox. Format it so someone can complete it in under 10 minutes. Topic: [TOPIC]. Audience: [AUDIENCE].
04

Package

Good content in poor packaging doesn't sell. Packaging is how buyers feel before they open it.

Packaging checklist

  • Outcome-driven product name — not vague or generic
  • "Read Me First" onboarding file written and included
  • Logical folder / module structure — easy to navigate
  • Format matched to how your audience actually consumes content
  • At least one 15-minute quick win early in the product
  • Clear "next step" at the end — don't leave buyers stranded

AI prompts

Product name brainstorm
Brainstorm 10 name options for a [PRODUCT_TYPE] that helps [AUDIENCE] achieve [OUTCOME]. Make names specific, outcome-driven, and memorable. Mix styles: some descriptive, some aspirational, some framework-named. Flag the top 3 with a reason why they'd stand out in a crowded market.
"Read Me First" onboarding writer
Write a friendly "Read Me First" document for my [PRODUCT_TYPE] for [AUDIENCE] on [TOPIC]. Welcome the buyer, explain what's included, tell them exactly where to start, give one tip for getting the most value, and set expectations for what they'll achieve. Tone: encouraging, clear, and human. Under 200 words.

The sale

Sales checklist

  • Core product transformation clearly stated above the fold
  • At least one bonus that speeds up the core result
  • Price anchored to time or outcome saved — not just cost
  • At least one before/after example or social proof
  • 5-post launch campaign planned and ready

AI prompts

Sales page copy prompt
Write a short sales description for my [PRODUCT_TYPE] for [AUDIENCE]. Their main struggle: [PAIN]. The outcome they get: [OUTCOME]. Include: a headline, a 2-sentence hook, what's inside (3–5 bullets), who it's for, and a call to action. Tone: confident, warm, direct. Avoid hype — speak to outcomes, not excitement.
5-post social launch campaign
Create a 5-post social media campaign to launch my [PRODUCT_TYPE] on [TOPIC] for [AUDIENCE]. Use these 5 angles: (1) problem/pain, (2) result/transformation, (3) what's inside, (4) objection-busting, (5) final call to action. Each post 3–5 sentences, conversational, written for Instagram. No clichés, no hype. Outcome buyers get: [OUTCOME].
06

Scale

Scaling isn't more hustle — it's systems, offers, and leverage working together.

The 3 scale pillars

⚙️Delivery systemsAutomated fulfillment — files, access, onboarding
📣Marketing systemsContent + email engines — always-on promotion
🪜Offer systemsLadders + upsells — starter to premium

Retention checklist

  • Design a complementary product that's a natural next step
  • Write a post-purchase nurture email sequence (3 emails minimum)
  • Ask buyers "where did you get stuck?" — let data drive your next product
  • Offer an upgrade or continuity product at or after purchase
  • Update products periodically — keep them current and valuable

AI prompts

Post-purchase email sequence
Write a 3-email post-purchase sequence for buyers of my [PRODUCT_TYPE] on [TOPIC]. Email 1 (sent immediately): welcome + how to start. Email 2 (sent day 3): implementation tip + encouragement. Email 3 (sent day 7): check-in + introduce my next product. Tone: personal, warm, coach-like. Each email under 150 words. Subject lines included. Audience: [AUDIENCE].
Ecosystem planner prompt
I sell a [PRODUCT_TYPE] on [TOPIC] to [AUDIENCE]. Help me design a 4-product ecosystem that takes my buyer from beginner to advanced. For each product, suggest: a product type, a working title, the transformation it delivers, and how it connects to the product before and after it. Include free, entry, core, and premium tiers. Make each feel like a natural next step.
